Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a trip?

You can book a trip by clicking the "Book Your Trip" button on our website to open the booking form. Fill in your details, and our travel experts will contact you shortly to finalize your itinerary and payment.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept various payment methods, including credit/debit cards, net banking, and popular UPI platforms. All transactions are secure and encrypted.

Can I customize my tour package?

Absolutely! We specialize in creating personalized travel experiences. Contact our team, and we will work with you to build a custom itinerary that matches your interests and budget perfectly.

What is your cancellation policy?

Our cancellation policy varies depending on the destination and services booked. Generally, you can find detailed information in the "Terms & Services" link in our footer or in the booking confirmation document.

Do you provide visa assistance?

Yes, we provide guidance and assistance with the visa application process for many countries. However, the final decision to grant a visa rests with the respective embassy or consulate.
